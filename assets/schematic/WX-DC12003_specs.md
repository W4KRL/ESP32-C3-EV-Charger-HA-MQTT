# WX-DC12003 - Switching power supply

The WX-DC12003 is an open-type switch-mode converter with a wide input voltage range from 70 to 277 V.
The output voltage of 5 V and a current of 0.7A.

## Product features

- Ultra-thin, ultra-small
- Isolated Switching Power Supply
- All voltage input (AC: 85 \~ 265V)
- DC voltage output 5v, 3.5W Output
- Low ripple and low noise
- High efficiency, high power density
- Low power consumption, environmental protection, no-load loss <0.1W

## Specifications

- Product Code WX-DC12003
- AC Input Voltage Range AC 50V-277V
- DC Input Voltage Range DC 70V-390V
- Output voltage 5 V ±0.15 V
- Output current up to 700mA
- No-load power <0.05W
- Power at full load <4 W
- Operation indicator LED
- Efficiency \~80%
